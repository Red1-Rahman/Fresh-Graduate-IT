# Fresh Graduate IT

Open-source preparation and learning materials for fresh graduates and students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Information Systems, and related fields.

Fresh Graduate IT is a community-driven collection of study guides, admission preparation materials, practice questions, career preparation resources, and reference materials for students and fresh graduates entering higher education, competitive examinations, academia, or the technology industry.

The repository includes preparation materials for opportunities such as MSc in CSE and related postgraduate admission tests, BCS and other competitive examinations, lecturer recruitment, technical interviews, and entry-level IT careers. Materials are provided as editable LaTeX source files together with their compiled PDF versions, making them easy to read, reuse, improve, and contribute to.

The project is designed to grow through community contributions. Students, graduates, educators, engineers, researchers, and other contributors are welcome to improve existing materials, correct errors, create new resources, and share useful knowledge with others.

## What You Can Find Here

* MSc and postgraduate admission preparation
* BCS and competitive examination preparation
* Lecturer and academic recruitment preparation
* Software engineering and IT career preparation
* Computer Science fundamentals
* Programming, algorithms, and data structures
* Computer networks and operating systems
* Databases and computer architecture
* Artificial intelligence and machine learning
* Interview and aptitude preparation
* Study guides, notes, practice questions, and reference materials
* Accessible and colorblind-friendly diagrams

The repository is intended for learners from CSE, CS, Software Engineering, IT, CIS, IS, ITM, and other related computing and information technology disciplines.

## Open Source and Community Driven

Fresh Graduate IT is built as an open educational resource. The goal is to make useful preparation materials freely available while allowing the community to continuously improve them.

If you find an error, have better explanations, want to add questions, or want to create preparation material for an examination or career path that is not yet covered, contributions are welcome.
